How to play

First to seven points wins. Keep the ball past the CPU paddle and defend your side of the court.

  1. Press Start match to serve.
  2. Move your paddle with the mouse or touch, or with W/S and ↑/↓.
  3. The ball picks up speed with every paddle hit.
  4. Pick Easy, Normal or Hard before the match to change how quickly the CPU reacts.
  5. The current and best rally are shown below the court.
  6. Press P or Space to pause at any time.
